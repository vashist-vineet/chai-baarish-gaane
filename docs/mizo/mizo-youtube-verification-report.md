## 2. Verification Architecture & Provenance Standards
### A. Provenance Hierarchy
In accordance with CBG catalog guidelines, candidate recordings were selected using a strict provenance hierarchy:
1. **Official Artist Channel / Verified Label:** Directly from the verified artist channel (e.g. *Guru Gee*, *Rebecca Lallawmsangi*, *SaiWanah Music*, *Kimkima Official*, *K. Hminga*, *Vanlalsailova Official*, *Bethsy Lalrinsangi*, *Jonathan Lianhna*, *Maruati Hnamte*, *Hlimhlimi*) or official regional publisher (*Zorimawi*, *YK Entertainment*, *Blue Wave Records*, *Westend Studio*).
2. **Official YouTube Topic Channels:** Auto-generated distributor feeds for official digital single/album releases (e.g., *Triau Trackx - Topic*, *Mizo Classic - Topic*, *Manuna - Topic*, *Mimi Renthlei - Topic*, *Michael M Sailo - Topic*).
3. **Cultural / Institutional Archives & Heritage Digitizations:** Pu Rokunga's cultural heritage compositions and golden-era classics were verified through cultural association broadcasts by the *Central YMA Zaipawl* and *YMA Branch Zaipawl*, regional media archives (*Mamit Vision Official*), community archives (*Mizo Zotui Thiang*), or heritage cassette digitizations (*Marcus Zaua*).
4. **Zero Collisions & Zero Duplicates:** Every single 11-character video ID is unique within this 100-song catalog and exhibits 0 collisions with the 1,265 active tracks (1,263 populated IDs) in `playlist.js`.

### B. Availability & Playback Status Definitions
- **Verified match:** The candidate represents the exact intended song and recording, and has passed Tier 1 YouTube oEmbed verification (HTTP 200, valid title, public author, embed-eligible).
- **Probable match:** The recording appears correct, but minor identity details remain under review.
- **Unavailable:** The video is removed, private, region-blocked, or returns an HTTP error on oEmbed.
- **Unresolved:** No sufficiently reliable match could be identified.
- **Playback test:** Tracked strictly and separately:
  - `Passed (Chromium live test)`: Tested live in an isolated Chromium browser session via the YouTube iframe player API, confirming advancement to State 1 (`PLAYING`) with `currentTime > 0.15s`.
  - `Not tested (Tier 1 oEmbed verified)`: Confirmed public, active, and embed-eligible via YouTube oEmbed; awaiting full automated batch playback testing during Phase 3.
